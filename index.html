About NovaFit

Coaching built around your actual week, not an ideal one.

NovaFit started in 2018 as a single studio room with eight members and a shared belief: fitness plans fail when they ignore real life. Every program we run today still starts with the same question — what does this person's week actually look like?

6Years coaching
9Certified coaches

What sets our coaching apart

  • Programs adjust weekly based on how you're actually recovering, not a fixed calendar.
  • Every member gets a coach who knows their history, injuries, and goals by name.
  • Classes stay small on purpose — twelve members, never a crowd of forty.
